Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Sivarr sets two cookies, both strictly necessary.

1 The short version

Sivarr sets two cookies. Both are necessary for the app to work and neither is used for advertising. We do not use tracking cookies, we do not sell data, and there is no third-party ad network anywhere in Sivarr.

2 The cookies we set

Cookie Purpose Lifetime
__Host-sivarr_session Keeps you signed in. Cannot be read by JavaScript. 30 days
sivarr_csrf Stops other websites making changes to your account while you are signed in. Session

Both are marked Secure and SameSite, so they are only sent over HTTPS and are not attached to requests originating from other sites.

3 Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count page views. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect or store any personal data or cross-site identifiers. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is no identifier following you around.

4 Error reporting

We use Sentry to capture application errors so we can fix them. Sentry receives technical details about the error and the browser it happened in. It does not set advertising cookies.

5 Things stored in your browser

Separately from cookies, Sivarr keeps some data in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, for example your theme preference, your sidebar layout, and a cached copy of your tasks and habits so the app opens instantly and keeps working on a poor connection. This never leaves your device except when syncing your own data to your own account.

Clearing your browser data for Sivarr removes all of it.

6 Controlling cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the two cookies above will stop you being able to sign in, because they are what keep you signed in and what keep your account safe.
