Security

How Sivarr protects your account

Last updated: August 2026  ·  This page describes measures that are live in the product today.

1 Your password

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before they are stored. We never store, log, or transmit your password in plaintext, and nobody at Sivarr can read it. If you sign in with Google, we never see a password at all.

2 Two-factor authentication

You can turn on two-factor authentication in Settings. It uses standard TOTP codes, so it works with Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, or any other authenticator app. When you enable it we give you one-time recovery codes; store them somewhere safe, because they are how you get back in if you lose your phone.

3 How your session is kept

Signing in sets a session cookie that JavaScript cannot read (HttpOnly), that is only ever sent over HTTPS in production, and that is scoped to Sivarr alone. Sessions expire after 30 days. Signing out clears the session on our servers, not just in your browser, so a copied cookie stops working.

4 Protecting your account from other sites

Every action that changes your data is protected by a CSRF token, so another website cannot make requests to Sivarr on your behalf while you are signed in. We also send strict browser security headers: a Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options to stop Sivarr being embedded in a hostile page, nosniff, and HSTS to force HTTPS for a year.

5 Connected accounts

When you connect Google Calendar or another service, the access tokens are encrypted at rest with Fernet (AES) before being written to the database. Disconnecting a service revokes the token with that provider, not just on our side.

6 Rate limiting and abuse

Sign-in attempts, password resets, and AI requests are all rate limited. The limiter is shared across every server instance, so it cannot be bypassed by retrying against a different one.

7 Deleting things

Deleting a task, habit, goal, note, or document moves it to Trash rather than destroying it immediately, so you can restore it. Items left in Trash are permanently purged after 30 days by an automated job.

8 Our infrastructure

Sivarr runs on Railway with a managed PostgreSQL database on Supabase. Row-level security is enabled on every table. Errors are reported to Sentry so we find problems before you have to tell us. All traffic is served over HTTPS.

9 Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security problem, please email security@sivarr.com with enough detail to reproduce it. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. We will confirm we have received your report and keep you updated.

We do not currently run a paid bug bounty, but we will credit you if you would like to be named.
