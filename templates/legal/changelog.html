Changelog

What is new in Sivarr. Newest first.

Sivarr AI now knows your workspace August 2026

  • Ask Sivarr AI about your own tasks, notes, and goals and it answers from your actual workspace instead of guessing.
  • Your data is only ever searched for you. Nobody else's workspace is ever visible to your assistant.

Two-factor authentication August 2026

  • Turn on 2FA in Settings using any authenticator app.
  • One-time recovery codes so you are not locked out if you lose your phone.

Bring your work with you August 2026

  • Import a Notion export and your pages become notes, your databases become tasks.
  • Import from Trello or Asana. Lists and sections carry over as task statuses, due dates come with them.
  • Find it under Settings, next to the existing CSV import.

Search that actually finds things August 2026

  • Search now covers tasks, goals, notes, documents, journal entries, org documents and messages, community posts, skills, and finance records.
  • Results are ranked by relevance rather than listed in whatever order they were found.
  • Deleted items no longer show up in results.

A faster app August 2026

  • The app now ships a compressed build, cutting roughly a third off what your browser has to download.
  • Goals load instantly from a local cache instead of waiting for the network.

Trash and restore August 2026

  • Deleting a task, habit, goal, note, or document now moves it to Trash instead of destroying it.
  • Restore anything from the Trash panel. Items are permanently removed after 30 days.

Focus sessions sync August 2026

  • Focus sessions recorded on mobile now sync to your account, so your history follows you between devices.

Real-time collaboration August 2026

  • See who else is online in your organisation, live.
  • Edit an organisation document at the same time as a teammate without overwriting each other.
  • Task changes appear for everyone in the space as they happen, no refresh needed.

Organisation and Academic spaces August 2026

  • A rebuilt Organisation space with projects, team goals, announcements, and analytics.
  • A rebuilt Academic space for classes, quizzes, flashcards, and study planning.
