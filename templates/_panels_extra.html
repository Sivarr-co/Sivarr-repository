How can we help?

Answers, guides, and a direct line to the Sivarr team.

Frequently asked

How do I invite people to my organisation?

Open your Org space → Members → Invite, enter their email and role. They'll get an email plus an in-app notification with a one-click join link.

How do seats work?

Each member takes one seat. Buy seats up front from Org billing (choose any number, volume discounts apply), then invite up to that many members — add more anytime.

Can I use Sivarr on my phone?

Yes. Sivarr is an installable PWA — open it in your mobile browser and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Is my data private?

Your data is yours. Export everything anytime from Settings → Data & Privacy, and delete your account whenever you like.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password” — we'll email you a one-click reset link that expires in an hour.

Still need a hand?
Our team usually replies within a day.
