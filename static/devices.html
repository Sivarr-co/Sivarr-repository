Sivarr· Device Preview

Frames load /app same-origin, so this only works when opened from the app's own domain (e.g. https://sivarr.com/static/devices.html or your local server) — not as a file://. Use the rotate (⟳) button per device to flip portrait/landscape. For true touch/DPR/network-throttling emulation, also use your browser's device mode (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+M) on a single frame.
